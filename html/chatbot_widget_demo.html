Chatbot widget demo

This page only exists to test the widget on its own. Click the round button in the bottom-right corner to open it.

To add the chatbot to any other page in the site, add these two lines — the widget builds and inserts itself, no extra markup needed:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/css/chatbot_widget.css">

<script src="/js/chatbot_widget.js"></script> (place before </body>)

The bot avatar uses /images/logo.png — update the AVATAR_SRC constant at the top of chatbot_widget.js if your file has a different name or extension.
