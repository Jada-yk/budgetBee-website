NEEDS VS WANTS

Hi, it's me again — BudgetBee! Now let's test what you know. I'll show you 8 everyday expenses and you decide: is it a NEED or a WANT? Pick an answer for each one, and I'll tell you right away whether you got it and why. Ready? Let's go!

[ ] NEED

Something you can't reasonably do without it keeps you fed, housed, healthy, or able to earn a living.

  • Rent or housing costs
  • Food and groceries
  • Transport to work or school
  • Medicine and healthcare

[ ] WANT

Something nice to have that makes life more enjoyable, but you could still get by without it.

  • Newest gadgets and upgrades
  • Eating out and entertainment
  • Designer or branded items
  • Streaming and other subscriptions
