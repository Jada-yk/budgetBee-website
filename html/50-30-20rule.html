THE 50-30-20 RULE

Hi, BudgetBee here again! Now that you know needs from wants, let's talk about a simple way to split any income: the 50-30-20 rule. Try it below with your own numbers.

A simple, effective way to divide your income

  • 50% Needs Essentials like rent, bills, groceries, and transportation.
  • 30% Wants Fun and leisure, including dining out, shopping, and entertainment.
  • 20% Savings Future goals, emergency funds, and debt repayment.
