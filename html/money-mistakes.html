COMMON MONEY MISTAKES TO AVOID

Hey, BudgetBee again! Even a good budget can leak money through a few common habits. Here are four to watch for — and what to do instead.

Lifestyle Creep

Spending increases as income grows, so savings never actually catch up.

vs.

Save Your Raises

Automate a portion of every raise straight into savings before you get used to it.

Impulse Buying

Frequent, unbudgeted small purchases made without a second thought.

vs.

Use a 24-Hour Wait Rule

Delay any purchase over $50 by a day — most urges pass on their own.

Minimum Payments

Paying only the minimum lets high-interest credit card debt keep accumulating.

vs.

Pay Off Balance in Full

Clearing the full balance monthly avoids interest charges entirely.

No Emergency Buffer

Overspending on non-essentials leaves nothing set aside before an emergency hits.

vs.

Build 3–6 Months of Essentials

A cash buffer creates real financial security when the unexpected happens.
