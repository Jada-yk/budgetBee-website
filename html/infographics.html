50-30-20 Budget Split

  • 50% Needs — rent, bills, groceries, transport
  • 30% Wants — dining out, shopping, entertainment
  • 20% Savings — emergency fund, goals, debt payoff

Savings

Emergency Fund Step-Ladder

1

Starter Fund

A small cash cushion (e.g. one month's transport + food) to absorb the first surprise expense.

2

1 Month of Essentials

Enough saved to cover a full month of needs if income is delayed or interrupted.

3

3 Months of Essentials

A solid buffer that covers most short-term emergencies without touching credit.

4

6 Months of Essentials

Full financial security — enough to ride out a longer gap in income.
