BUDGETING BASICS

Hi I'm BudgetBee. Let's learn where your money comes from, where it goes, and look at a real example! Here is how a student might plan a ₦100,000 income.

[ ] INCOME

[ ] FIXED vs VARIABLE EXPENSES

Fixed: Stays the same.
Variable: Costs that change

[ ] NEEDS vs WANTS

Need: Essential for life.
Want: Enhances enjoyment

[ ] SAVINGS

Money set aside for the future or emergencies
