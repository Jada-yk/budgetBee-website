About us

BudgetBasics is a student-focused financial learning platform designed to make understanding money simple, practical, and interactive. We help students learn the basics of budgeting, saving, managing expenses, and making smarter everyday financial decisions through simple lessons, interactive activities, and hands-on tools.

From learning the difference between needs and wants to planning expenses and setting savings goals, BudgetBasics turns financial concepts into practical skills you can use in everyday life.

Learn. Try. Understand. Apply.

BudgetBasics is an educational project and is not intended to provide professional financial advice.
